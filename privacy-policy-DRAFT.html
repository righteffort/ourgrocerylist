Privacy Policy — Our Grocery List

Last updated: April 26, 2026

Our Grocery List is a household shopping list app. This policy describes what data the app collects and how it is used.

Data we collect

  • Account information. Sign-in is handled by Firebase Authentication. We store your email address and a unique user ID to identify your account.
  • Shopping list content. List names, item names, quantities, and checked/unchecked status are stored in Google Firestore under your user ID.
  • Shared-list collaborators. If you share a list with another user, their email address is stored on that list document so you can see who has access.

How we use your data

Your data is used solely to operate the app: storing your lists, syncing them across your devices, and enabling real-time collaboration with people you explicitly invite. We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, or any purpose beyond providing the service.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with any third parties.

Data retention and deletion

Your data is retained as long as your account exists. To delete your account and all associated data, contact us at the email address below. We will delete your account and all lists you own within 30 days.

Security

Access to your lists is restricted to you and users you have explicitly added as editors.

Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: support@righteffort.org
